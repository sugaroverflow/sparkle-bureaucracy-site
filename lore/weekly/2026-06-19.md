## 3.2 Decisions Made

- Keep GitHub as the canonical project diary and source of record.
- Use a separate commentable surface, likely Google Docs, when faculty feedback needs inline comments or lower friction.
- Treat the agent-assisted diary as a possible prototype experiment, but constrain it to drafting, triage, and suggestions unless human-approved.
