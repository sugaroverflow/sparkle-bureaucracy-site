# Weeks of 6 & 13 July — Campaign Factory and the AI & Campaigning Conference

*Reconstructed 31 July 2026 from the git record.*

## What happened

- **6–11 July** — Topic (timetable) development continues at a low hum (~9 commits).
- **12–17 July** — a very intense sprint (~122 commits, 60 of them on 15 July alone) builds **Campaign Factory** (`CampaignLab/campaign-factory`): give it a UK local or public-policy problem, a named place, and your own Anthropic/OpenRouter API key, and it researches the decision route, maps power and pressure, proposes strategy and tactics, and drafts campaign materials — *showing the work as it happens*. Live at campaign-factory.vercel.app.
- **15–16 July** — **Conference Pulse** (`CampaignLab/conference-pulse`) built in parallel: the live companion site for Campaign Lab's **AI & Campaigning Conference, 16 July at Newspeak House** — schedule, anonymous per-panel comments, votes, an organiser dashboard, and a big-screen room view.

## Sparkle Bureaucracy reading

Both projects are the SB pattern applied outside the fellowship bubble, with a partner community (Campaign Lab): a serious procedural skeleton (campaign planning's decision routes and power maps; a conference's schedule and Q&A) made legible and participatory, with the work shown rather than hidden. Campaign Factory later headlines the "projects" list in the July site outline. This is also the calendar/alliances strategy in action — building *with* an adjacent community rather than only under the SB banner.
