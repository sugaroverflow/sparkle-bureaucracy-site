# Week of 22 June — prospective experiments, and Topic begins

*Reconstructed 31 July 2026 from the git record.*

## What happened

- **23 June (evening)** — `prospective-experiments/README.md` lands in the diary repo (uncommitted, 18KB): the working idea bank for future SB experiments. It tightens the definition of what counts — an experiment needs all four of **institutional skeleton, affective re-skin, decision pressure, durable artifact** — and ranks a shortlist of nine: Public Voice Customs, Mirror Commons, The Exception Window, Permission Passport Office, The Value Court, Claw Civil Service, Shadow Labour Claims Office, Liquid Mandate Assembly, and the Sparkle Border Field Kit. Top canonical-potential candidates: Public Voice Customs ("pol.is for synthetic-voice-era consultations") and Mirror Commons (consentful synthetic representation).
- **23 June (same day)** — the **Topic** build kicks off: 43 commits to `sugaroverflow/timetable` in one day, continuing through the end of the month (~66 commits by 30 June). Topic (topic.forum, formerly "Timetable") is collaborative forum software for proposing topics, voting with hearts, sharing availability, and shaping a schedule together — billed in its README as *"a Newspeak House × Sparkle Bureaucracy production."*

## Decisions visible in the record

- The experiment bar was raised: "if an idea does not change who can act, how judgment is made, what becomes visible, or how a decision can be contested, it is probably not strong enough yet."
- Rather than one of the nine shortlisted experiments, the thing that actually got built next was **Topic** — participatory scheduling infrastructure. The record doesn't say whether that was a deliberate choice over the shortlist or a response to a concrete Newspeak need that arrived with its own momentum.
