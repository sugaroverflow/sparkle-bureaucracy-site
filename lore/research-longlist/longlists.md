# Linked Longlists

Research date: 30 July 2026
Status: curated desk-research longlists

The three discovery passes produced 50 project profiles, 49 organisation candidates, 41 directories or archives, and 47 people or networks before deduplication. The lists below are the editorial cut to 25 of each.

Projects are the primary research unit. Project IDs connect the lists; the linked discovery profiles contain the fuller evidence record, including designed affect, any available reception evidence, power, contestability, risks, and source confidence.

## Coverage check

- **Affect:** relief, dignity, belonging, moral discomfort, frustration, trust, empathy, suspense, certainty and productive disagreement.
- **Forms of institution:** courts, borders, benefits offices, hospitals, hiring, grants, planning, immigration, assemblies and care organisations.
- **Technology:** no-tech and low-tech process design, mobile and SMS, open digital infrastructure, foundation-dataset investigation, automation, LLM synthesis, generative imagery and multi-agent/collective-intelligence research.
- **Geography:** Europe, North America, Latin America, South Asia, Australia, Aotearoa New Zealand and transnational/crisis contexts.
- **Power:** projects that remove gates, share allocation, make interpretation inspectable, create appeal, preserve an asymmetric status quo, or fail at the institutional handoff.

The longlist deliberately retains evidence gaps, but missing audience research is not missing Sparkle. Designed affect is assessed through the artifact and encounter; reception evidence is recorded separately when available. A project belongs in “infrastructure awaiting Sparkle” only when affective design itself is weak, absent, or inaccessible—not merely because no survey or participant quotation was found.
